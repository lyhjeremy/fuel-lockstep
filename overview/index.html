The question

Same price everywhere: competition or coordination?

In Los Angeles or Perth, stations a few hundred meters apart often post prices tens of cents a litre apart. In Hong Kong the five brands that run nearly every station post one price for days or weeks, then change it within a day or two of each other. A small market with the same import costs and fully public prices can produce that with no agreement at all. So can coordination. Competition law treats the two differently, and the tools for telling them apart are screens: tests of whether prices behave the way costs and independent decisions would make them behave.

This project runs those screens on every posted price change since April 2020 and puts two markets beside Hong Kong: Los Angeles, with many sellers and weekly federal price data, and Perth, where every station's price is public a day ahead.

Data

Six years of snapshots, rebuilt

The Consumer Council publishes each brand's posted price as a small file on data.gov.hk, and the portal keeps a copy every time the file changes: 1,210 versions since 30 April 2020. Rebuilt day by day, they give each brand's price on every day. Walk-in discounts are not in that file, so they come from 119 Internet Archive captures of the Council's comparison page; on those days its pump prices match the open-data file for 97.5% of rows. Hong Kong's import cost is the Census and Statistics Department's monthly unit value of unleaded petrol imports. Perth is FuelWatch's daily price for every metropolitan station; Los Angeles is the EIA's weekly retail and daily spot series.

Finding 1

A fixed order of leaders and followers

An episode starts with the first brand to change price and collects every other brand's first change in the same direction within seven days. In 90% of the 143 episodes all five moved, by the same amount in 83% of those. When one brand moved alone it was Shell 37 times, Esso 17 and Caltex 12; PetroChina and Sinopec almost never went first. The premium grade shows the same habit over a longer span: Shell raised its premium surcharge from HK$1.19 to HK$1.60 in January 2024, the others matched it in January 2025, Shell then went to HK$1.80, and all four matched within a week.

Finding 2

The margin doubled while the product did not

The posted price averaged HK$17.55 a litre in the first twelve months and HK$30.49 in the last twelve; the import cost went from HK$2.73 to HK$5.40. From January 2023 to December 2025 the posted price rose from HK$23.09 to HK$28.89 while the import cost fell from HK$5.23 to HK$4.29. Walk-in discounts grew too, from an average of HK$2.44 to HK$5.52, but the margin on the price after that discount still went from HK$6.74 to HK$13.11.

The comparison

Perth: just as public, far more crowded

Perth's retailers must give FuelWatch the next day's price by 2pm and hold it for 24 hours, so prices are as public as Hong Kong's. On a typical day the cheapest and dearest of its 419 stations are 38 cents a litre apart, and the most common price covers 23% of them. The weekly cycle, low on Tuesday and restored on Wednesday, is a form of coordination that Byrne and de Roos (2019) traced to price leadership, but it ends in a price war every week.

What it means

Grounds to ask, not a finding

None of this is evidence of an agreement. Hong Kong's Competition Commission said in 2017 that follow-the-leader pricing on its own reveals little, and US law asks for more than parallel conduct before inferring a conspiracy. What the screens add is that a stable leader, increases that outnumber cuts three to one, faster pass-through of rises, and a margin that doubled while the product did not all occur together and persist. Station costs and rents, the timing of each brand's price notices, and dated discount terms would separate cost from conduct. None of them is published as data.

Limits: the record starts in April 2020; day-level timing depends on when the archive copied the file; the import price is a monthly average; the margin is gross of storage, delivery, station costs and rent; card and loyalty discounts are not observed.

Method and code

Reproduce it
