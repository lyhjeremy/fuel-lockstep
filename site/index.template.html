Where the money goes

Hong Kong imports all its petrol, and the duty has been HK$6.06 a litre since 1998. What is left after the import cost and the duty pays for storage, delivery, stations, land and profit. What a crowded, transparent market looks like

Perth's retailers must tell the state's FuelWatch service their next day's price by 2pm and hold it for 24 hours, so every price is public, as in Hong Kong. The difference is the number of sellers. On a typical day the cheapest and dearest of Perth's {{perthStations}} metro stations are {{perthRange}} cents a litre apart, and the most common price is posted by only {{perthModal}}% of stations. Prices run a weekly cycle: the median is lowest on Tuesday ({{perthTue}} cents) and jumps on Wednesday ({{perthWed}} cents), the day prices are restored. That cycle is coordination of its own kind (Byrne and de Roos 2019), and it still ends in a price war every week.

What this shows, and what it does not

None of this is evidence of an agreement. Identical prices are what a few sellers with the same costs and fully public prices would produce on their own, and Hong Kong's Competition Commission said in 2017 that this kind of parallel pricing reveals very little by itself. US law asks for more than parallel conduct before inferring a conspiracy (Bell Atlantic v. Twombly, 2007), and Hong Kong's First Conduct Rule likewise targets agreements and concerted practices.

What the screens add is that the patterns come together and last: a stable leader and followers, increases that outnumber cuts more than three to one, faster pass-through of rises than falls, and a margin that took HK${{marginRise}} of the HK${{postedRise}} rise in the posted price. The data that would separate cost from conduct are station operating costs and rents, the timing of each brand's price notices, and the full discount terms by date. The Consumer Council collects some of these; none are published as data.

Limits: the open-data record starts in April 2020. Day-level timing depends on when the archive copied the file. The import price is a monthly average. The margin is gross of storage, delivery, station costs and rent.
